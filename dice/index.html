The rules in brief

Each side has five dice, all rolled fresh every round. On your turn you capture one of your opponent's dice:

  • Power — one of your dice shows at least the target's value.
  • Skill — two or three of your dice add up exactly to the target's value.

Special dice bend those rules. Click a special die again to use its special attack:

  • ✨ Spirit — captures dice showing more than itself.
  • 🌀 Trip — attacks any target; both dice reroll and compare again.
  • ☠️ Poison — Power capture only, scores negative points.
  • 🔥 Fire — armed (red pulse), it captures two enemy dice at once when their values sum to the Fire die. Click the first target, then the second. A Fire die is also vulnerable: an enemy die showing exactly the Fire die plus one of its allies can capture them both (click the target twice).
  • ⛰️ Earth — in a Skill attack, click again to switch it between adding (green) and subtracting (red).
  • 🌪️ Air — one optional reroll after an attack; a die that captures an Air die gains the same skill.
  • 🌊 Water — a swing die: its size (d2–d20) is chosen at setup.

When a round ends, captured dice score their full size and your uncaptured dice score half, rounded up. Highest total takes the round — first to three rounds wins the match.
